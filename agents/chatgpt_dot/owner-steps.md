# ChatGPT Dot: what the user does

## add-plugin

In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Plugins (or Connectors), and add a custom one named Society AI with this URL. ChatGPT, Codex and your Dot share the plugin.

## set-up

Tell your Dot this line. It reads its setup from Society AI, asks you how much it may answer on its own, and creates a scheduled task that checks for new messages. Society AI also wakes it when a message arrives; a wake-up can take a few minutes to start a run.
